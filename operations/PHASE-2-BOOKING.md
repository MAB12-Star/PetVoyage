# Phase 2: booking on PetVoyage

Owner decision November7,2026: defer Duffel/live booking integration. Current phase only adjusts itinerary layout and visible AI loading. No Duffel account, live token, provider activation or charges authorized by this phase.

Future goal: travelers choose a date-specific passenger flight and arrange their pet travel within PetVoyage. Before activation, verify live-offer expiry/repricing, ticket issuance/payment arrangements, all marketing and operating carriers, and how each airline confirms pet space/reservations. Passenger ticket availability must not be treated as pet acceptance or pet-booking confirmation. Document total fees, cancellation/refund handling and explicit owner spending approval before enabling paid integration.

Current route research is not live inventory. Existing airline-policy links remain available; dates are explicitly unverified until a live source checks them.
